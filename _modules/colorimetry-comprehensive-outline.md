The series follows a signal from light to a displayed image.

1. [Light and vision]({{ '/notes/colorimetry/module-1/' | relative_url }}): spectra, metamerism, photoreceptors, and the observer integral.
2. [Measuring color]({{ '/notes/colorimetry/module-2/' | relative_url }}): matching experiments, XYZ, chromaticity, Lab, and film density.
3. [The image pipeline]({{ '/notes/colorimetry/module-3/' | relative_url }}): reference domains, models, transfer functions, and rendering.
4. [ACES]({{ '/notes/colorimetry/module-4/' | relative_url }}): encodings, gamut handling, and reproducible workflows.
5. [Displays]({{ '/notes/colorimetry/module-5/' | relative_url }}): PQ/HLG, surround, metadata, and system color management.
6. [Cameras]({{ '/notes/colorimetry/module-6/' | relative_url }}): LogC, working spaces, and RAW mosaics.
7. [Transforms]({{ '/notes/colorimetry/module-7/' | relative_url }}): fitting, interpolation, and limits of inversion.

The [mathematical appendix]({{ '/notes/colorimetry/appendix-mathematics/' | relative_url }}) collects equations and primary coordinates. Each figure names its source or states its illustrative assumptions.
