### About

I study how acute kidney injury triggers cascading effects across organ systems. My work focuses on tubuloglomerular feedback mechanisms and identifying severity thresholds where focal injuries create systemic consequences. Currently investigating multi-organ crosstalk using genetically engineered mouse models, large animal systems (porcine), and translational approaches.
